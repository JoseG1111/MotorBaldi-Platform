# ADR 0030 — Customer Free/Premium and vehicle services

Status: ACCEPTED by owner directive, 2026-10-11.

Free is permanent COP0 with one active owner vehicle and five manual maintenance entries. Premium monthly COP29,900 or annual COP288,000 provides identical benefits, three active owner vehicles and unlimited reasonable manual maintenance. Premium eligible assisted requests have no numeric quota. Staffing is approved; no 24/7 or regulated/third-party outcomes are promised. This supersedes earlier finite support-usage/default-two-vehicle and unresolved-staffing assumptions in Phase5/6, without activating external providers.

Shared typed customer policy supplies prices, limits and comparison metadata. Existing billing plans retain canonical codes; immutable billing-period price snapshots are preserved. Customer entitlement derives only from current canonical verified payment confirmation or authorized timed administrative grant, with active account/person and non-suspended/non-expired subscription. Pending requests never activate Premium. Cancellation preserves legitimate paid coverage through period end. Neither organization nor staff roles imply customer benefits.

Server authorization and transaction predicates enforce owner limits and support purposes. Downgrade retains canonical vehicle/maintenance/compliance/history and makes excess owner vehicles read-only; workshop organizational operations retain independent permissions. User-declared registration/expiry information is never official ownership/insurance/RTM verification. Compliance and manual history are append-only with audit/outbox; date-only expiry uses Colombia calendar semantics.

Free retains privacy, security, account and complaints channels; personalized technical and vehicle assistance require Premium. Historical support remains readable/replyable/closable after expiry. Assisted vehicle cases include immutable authorized covered owner vehicle context. Official interactive WhatsApp https://wa.me/573104602615 was verified in public motorbaldi.com HTML on 2026-10-11; availability is confirmed with the team, no schedule is invented. Clicking opens a real public channel; it is not an automatic notification API.

Reminder schedules Free15/Premium30,15,7,1 use notification preferences, verified contacts, current entitlement/grants, latest compliance revision, bounded cron scanning and the existing canonical outbox/Queue retry mechanism. Permanent provider unavailability is recorded honestly, never as sent/delivered. Existing notification external-delivery gate stays closed. Wompi verification, regulated transactions, automated external messaging and antimalware remain separately governed. No public checkout or manual customer-controlled entitlement activation is introduced.
