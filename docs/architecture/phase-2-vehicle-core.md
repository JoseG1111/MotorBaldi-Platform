# Phase 2 — Vehicle Core

Vehicle is a central business entity. Its UUIDv7 key is independent of a person, account, organization, garage entry, VIN, plate, or any other mutable identifier. A vehicle can therefore exist before an ownership or access claim is resolved. Garage visibility is not legal ownership.

The first Vehicle migration creates `vehicle_vehicles` with a bounded generic `kind_code` and object-shaped `specification_json`. It deliberately does not fix a road-vehicle-only taxonomy. A kind may be added without changing the table, and specifications carry descriptive attributes rather than identity or authorization. D1 enforces UUIDv7 shape, kind syntax, valid object JSON, positive version, and a one-step version increment on update. Application commands must still use optimistic compare-and-swap when they are introduced.

The next migration separates historical identifiers, explicit owner/driver/workshop relationships, pending relationship claims, access grants, and personal garage entries. Identifier history may only be retired; relationship history may only be ended; grants may only be revoked. A claim, relationship, plate, VIN, or garage entry grants no access by itself. The read-only `hasVehiclePermission` domain function requires an active account/person and an exact, unexpired, unrevoked grant. Organization grants additionally require an active organization and membership, with the grant location inside the membership's active location scope. No public vehicle lookup exists.

Odometer readings are append-only observations with a nonnegative integer value, explicit kilometers or miles unit, observation time, and recording account. A correction adds a linked reading for the same vehicle with a reason; it never overwrites the original. Professional records are generic drafts tied to a vehicle, organization, and author. Finalization freezes the original content and identity; subsequent corrections are append-only amendments to the final record. Detailed record fields and publication workflow remain outside this persistence contract.

The API now exposes read-only `GET /api/v1/vehicles/{vehicleId}` and `GET /api/v1/me/garage`. An unauthenticated request gets 401; a vehicle without an explicit current `vehicle.read` grant gets 404, including when it is bookmarked in a garage. The Portal presents only the filtered granted garage list. Vehicle writes follow the initial staff-reviewed policy below.

## Initial issuance and review policy

On 2026-10-09 the operator delegated remaining implementation choices and asked autonomous execution to proceed. The initial least-privilege policy uses the existing staff review pattern. This is a bounded Phase 2 policy, not a claim that previously missing product rules were present in the repository.

- `platform.vehicle.read` allows MFA-assured staff to inspect vehicles, identifier history, claims, relationships and grants in Admin. `platform.vehicle.manage` allows MFA-assured staff to create/update vehicles, retire identifiers, decide pending claims once, end relationships and issue/revoke exact grants. Initially only Superadmin and Operations Admin receive those permissions.
- A person with an explicit vehicle read grant can bookmark the vehicle and submit an OWNER or DRIVER claim for themselves. Staff acceptance creates the matching relationship atomically; it never creates an access grant or asserts government/legal title. No anonymous discovery by plate, VIN or UUID is available. Organization relationship claims are not self-service in this initial flow.
- Staff issues only the registered `vehicle.read`, `vehicle.odometer.write`, `vehicle.record.read` and `vehicle.record.write`, `vehicle.workshop.read` and `vehicle.workshop.write` grants, to active people or verified active organizations. Organization/location grants retain the membership/context restrictions in the access model. Professional operations and record/amendment reads additionally match the record organization/location, so an all-location member cannot use a grant limited to one location for another location. Garage removal remains available after access revocation without revealing whether an arbitrary vehicle exists.
- Odometer writers require an explicit exact grant; corrections append a same-vehicle link and reason. Future observation timestamps are rejected. This does not decide disputed legal mileage or enforce an assumed monotonically increasing mileage across corrections/units.
- Professional record authors require an explicit record-write grant plus an active, valid membership with `org.vehicle.record.manage` in a verified active organization and applicable location scope. Initially Owner, Admin, Mechanic and Inspector receive that organization permission. Only the author edits/finalizes/amends their records; finalization and amendments require assured MFA. Record reads require a separate exact record-read grant and show final records plus only the reader's own drafts. Final originals remain immutable.
- Every new vehicle command is serialized through the existing coordinator and batches its business changes, append-only audit, outbox event and D1 replay together. Conditional updates and replay collisions abort the batch through a dependent NOT NULL audit guard. The coordinator checks current session assurance and authorization before returning a vehicle replay. This closes the crash window only for these commands; existing generic coordinator debt remains for MB-P5-000.

Portal supplies garage, claim, odometer, professional draft/finalization/amendment flows. Admin supplies registration, specification updates, identifiers, reviewed claims, relationship end and grant issuance/revocation. Forms use Spanish labels, keyboard-accessible native controls and live status messages. UI permission visibility does not replace server authorization.

Exact kind taxonomy and mandatory specification fields, organization claim onboarding, legal ownership evidence standards, odometer dispute adjudication and phase-specific professional report fields remain **UNKNOWN — VERIFY FROM REPOSITORY**. The generic model does not silently define them. Workshop/Inspection phases must establish their own detailed contracts.
