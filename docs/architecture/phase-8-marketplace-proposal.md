# Phase 8 — Marketplace business contract proposal

Status: PROPOSED — owner decision required. This document grants no publication or commercial authority.

## Repository findings

[ADR 0026](../adr/0026-parts-catalog-and-phase-boundaries.md) explicitly requires a separate Phase 8 public/commercial contract. Phase 7 offerings are confidential within authorized organization/service workflows; ACTIVE does not mean consent to public publication. The existing `/api/v1/directory/organizations` exposes only verified active organizations' ID, display name, type and country, not offerings, commercial records, locations or private contacts. No marketplace listing, customer commercial order, quotation acceptance or fulfillment domain currently exists.

[Phase 5](phase-5-membership-billing.md) commissions require approved agreements and verified completed service evidence; those rules do not authorize parts commissions. Owner restrictions keep Wompi/external paid providers disabled. The real malware scanner is deferred; private quarantine cannot support public listing media. Existing general support cases do not grant partners customer support access or establish customer-to-seller messaging.

## Recommended approval package

Approve the following rules as one bounded Phase 8 baseline, or identify changes before implementation.

1. **Scope and readers.** Deliver public parts listing discovery and moderation, available to anonymous readers and authenticated customers. No customer commercial orders, seller inquiries/messaging, checkout, payments, inventory reservation/deduction, fulfillment, new fees/commissions or sourcing assistance in this baseline. These remain disabled pending a separate explicit contract. Existing authorized Workshop snapshots remain unchanged.
2. **Seller eligibility and consent.** Only ACTIVE VERIFIED PARTS-capable organizations may request publication of their own ACTIVE offerings. Location-specific offerings require an ACTIVE associated location and exact current location scope. Organization-wide offerings retain their existing management scope. Exact authorized organization OWNER/ADMIN members, with existing verified MFA and privileged-session assurance, explicitly submit a versioned publication snapshot. Existing private offerings are never automatically published. Organization suspension, verification/capability loss, offering inactivation or linked canonical archival removes discovery eligibility immediately.
3. **Public information.** Publish only reviewed product name, category, manufacturer/brand/reference, description, unit, optional COP integer-minor-unit price, availability/update timestamp, informational compatibility, seller public display name and country. Missing price displays “Consultar”; it does not create an inquiry workflow. Exclude partner SKU, internal IDs beyond opaque listing references, private contact/address information, vehicle identifiers, audit history, costs, margins and all other confidential commercial data. Description and other public free text must be reviewed for private information before publication. No media attachments or file URLs.
4. **Review and moderation.** Exact authorized CATALOG_ADMIN or PLATFORM_SUPERADMIN staff with verified MFA and privileged-session assurance review each submitted snapshot and may reject or suspend it with an audited reason. Organization users cannot approve their own submissions through organization authority. Proposed lifecycle: DRAFT → PENDING_REVIEW → PUBLISHED; rejected submissions return to DRAFT; published records may become SUSPENDED or ARCHIVED. Re-publication requires review. Publication history is immutable and referenced records are not destructively deleted.
5. **Changes and discovery.** Edits prepare a new draft snapshot requiring fresh review; they never silently alter an approved public snapshot. A previously approved snapshot may remain visible only while its source offering version and all eligibility conditions still match; a source version change hides it until a new snapshot is approved. Withdrawal is available to the authorized owning organization. Search uses approved public fields, bounded pagination and deterministic ordering without paid placement or commission-based ranking. Compatibility does not enable private VIN/plate lookup.
6. **Commercial claims and responsibility.** Prices and availability are partner-provided informational values, not accepted quotations or guaranteed real-time stock. Compatibility never guarantees fitment from vehicle model alone; workshops remain responsible for confirming fitment. MotorBaldi makes no diagnosis, roadside, response-time or SLA promise. This discovery-only baseline establishes no new transaction, warranty, returns, refund, settlement or legal liability terms. Those policies must be explicitly approved before enabling any transaction workflow; implementation cannot invent contractual disclaimers.
7. **Security and verification.** Use existing scoped authorization, current assurance, CAS, idempotency, audit and outbox patterns. Public reads expose only eligible reviewed snapshots; private source APIs keep existing authorization. Machine validation remains Development-only and restricted to its own synthetic resources through separately tested minimal permissions; it cannot approve or publish real partner data. Verify revoked authority, source/canonical changes, private-field exclusion, stale/replayed commands and genuine Development D1/audit/outbox/Queue processing before closing Phase 8 remote validation. No staging/production deployment is authorized by this package.

## Required owner action

Approve this discovery-only package as written, or specify the desired changes to public access, publication/review policy or commercial scope. If orders or inquiries are requested, their actors, lifecycle, privacy, fulfillment obligations and commercial terms require a concrete additional contract before coding.
