# Phase 7 — Partners, parts and assistance

## Established partner capability/relationship model

The accepted [master architecture](MOTORBALDI_MASTER.md), [identity/organization model](phase-1-identity-crm-organizations.md) and [ADR 0025](../adr/0025-membership-billing-commercial-boundaries.md) already establish partner participation as capabilities and relationships attached to an organization or professional. Partner is not an exclusive organization type.

An organization retains its existing type while holding several registered operational capabilities. For example, a WORKSHOP can also hold PARTS, INSPECTION and TOWING. `org_capabilities` stores organization/code pairs; the capability registry owns valid codes, and the composite primary key prevents duplicates. `org_location_capabilities` represents location-specific capabilities separately. Declaring a capability does not establish service availability, commercial approval or authorization to another account's vehicles or records.

Existing active organization membership and `org.capability.manage` authorize capability changes. Replacement is an atomic D1 batch with its audit event: invalid registry codes cannot erase the previous set. Capabilities do not create memberships, grant platform support access, enable payments or turn on premium assistance.

The existing versioned `commission_agreements` relationship attaches commercial terms to an organization without changing its type or capabilities. Agreements and their rules follow the approved [Phase 5 contract](phase-5-membership-billing.md): explicit authorized finance review, immutable approved terms, customer consent and verified completed-service evidence for recognition. A draft relationship does not imply an approved commission, settlement readiness, partner subscription charge or automatic payout. Organization capabilities remain independent of agreement versions and status.

Four real-D1 regressions verify membership denial, atomic rollback, combined capabilities and commercial relationship independence; full gate passes 514 tests. MB-P7-001 verifies this existing model rather than adding a parallel partner identity, exclusive PARTNER type or new commercial state machine. MB-P7-004 adds registered-code validation before capability replacement, returning a sanitized400 for unknown codes without changing prior data. The legacy capability PUT remains atomic and permission-scoped but has no CAS/replay contract; it declares capabilities without granting Parts data authority. All new Parts mutations independently recheck current role/MFA/verification/capability/location and vehicle grants at commit, with CAS and replay. Parts inventory and assistance fulfillment require their own business contracts before implementation.

## Approved Parts baseline — 2026-10-10

The owner approved [ADR 0026](../adr/0026-parts-catalog-and-phase-boundaries.md), superseding the earlier catalog-only proposal. MotorBaldi maintains canonical reference parts; verified partner organizations maintain independent offerings with organization ownership, optional location association and optional explicit canonical references.

Canonical data is managed only by authorized exact PLATFORM_SUPERADMIN/CATALOG_ADMIN staff with verified MFA and privileged-session assurance. Each reasoned canonical mutation is an authorized reviewed command. Offerings are managed by active organization OWNER/ADMIN members of their own verified active PARTS-capable organization under exact current organization/location scope and assurance. Changing an offering's location checks both previous and new scope; selected-location membership cannot modify organization-wide offerings. Canonical reference lookup is available to authorized offering managers inside their organization scope, for ACTIVE references only. There is no global customer marketplace/catalog discovery or cross-organization commercial read.

Required descriptive fields are product name, category, brand/manufacturer, manufacturer reference, description and unit. Offerings additionally support optional partner SKU and integer COP minor-unit price (null displays Consultar), informational UNKNOWN/AVAILABLE/UNAVAILABLE status and server update timestamp. Bounded compatibility references contain vehicle kind and optional brand/model/note; they remain informational/unverified and never include private VIN, plate or vehicle identity lookup. Workshops confirm fitment independently.

Controlled canonical identity is the normalized brand/manufacturer-reference pair. NFKC normalization, trimming, whitespace folding and case folding preserve separators; names do not identify a part. Exact strong-key conflicts require staff review of the existing reference, not automatic merge or destructive deduplication. Explicit offering links must match the canonical strong reference; activation of a linked offering requires an ACTIVE canonical record. Unlinked offerings are permitted where no canonical reference applies.

Canonical lifecycle is DRAFT/ACTIVE/ARCHIVED; offering lifecycle is DRAFT/ACTIVE/INACTIVE. Creation starts DRAFT. Subsequent content/state commands require current authority, version and a reason; approved policy does not invent terminal archival or prohibit authorized reviewed restoration. Independent version histories, audit, replay and minimal-ID outbox commit atomically; mutation-time SQL guards retain current account, MFA, role, verification, capability and location boundaries. Historical records are never destructively deleted.

Existing Workshop service workflows may record immutable part/price snapshots only for an ACTIVE offering owned by the same organization and applicable location. Existing exact Workshop management, vehicle write grants, current location scope and mutable-order version apply. The snapshot retains canonical/offering versions and descriptive/compatibility/price data independently of later catalog changes. Completed/closed/cancelled operations reject additions. Reads retain existing Workshop/vehicle authorization. No quotation domain currently exists: financial quotation acceptance, totals, checkout and stock operations are not invented by this integration. Historical quotations must adopt immutable part/price snapshots whenever an authorized quotation capability is later added.

The phase adds no stock reservation/deduction, direct parts payment, customer checkout, public marketplace, automatic parts commission or sourcing assistance. Private R2/quarantine restrictions remain unchanged; no Parts media publication or unscanned-file exception is introduced. Phase 8 requires separately approved discovery/order/payment/commission rules.

## Assistance boundaries

The [approved general-support workflow](phase-6-communications-support.md#approved-support-workflow--2026-10-10) remains available under its exact customer/staff authorization. Premium guidance remains disabled until configured operating hours, staffing, entitlement/usage limits and business readiness. TOWING capability does not authorize dispatch, roadside availability, diagnosis, guaranteed response times or an SLA. A separate service-request/fulfillment contract is still required if Phase 7 assistance extends beyond existing support cases.

The owner-directed Development-only automation exception in [ADR 0027](../adr/0027-development-automation-authentication.md) uses a distinct passwordless machine principal, fixed synthetic Workshop/site and owned synthetic resources. It does not assert human MFA or change human, staging or production authorization. Signed contexts, current least-privilege roles, scoped ownership and commit-time guards remain mandatory.
